# Awesome-Alternative-Investment-Platform

## Top Alternative Investment Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Private Equity & Venture Fund Operations, LP Portals, Portfolio Monitoring, Deal Flow & Alternative Asset Administration*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Alternative Investment Platforms**. These systems help GPs and LPs manage fundraising, deal flow, portfolio companies, capital calls, and reporting for private equity, venture, and other alternatives.



**Examples** include iCapital, CAIS, Juniper Square, Allvue Systems, Dynamo Software, Backstop Solutions, Chronograph, InvestX, AngelList, and Vestberry (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Alternative investment software is overwhelmingly commercial. Emerging open options include **fund operations platforms** (e.g. Portfolio/Hemrock) and **PE intelligence** stacks (e.g. OpenGP). This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Portfolio (Hemrock)](https://github.com/tdavidson/reporting)**  

  Open-source (Apache 2.0) operating platform for venture funds—deal flow, portfolio reporting, fund operations, LPs, and accounting; self-hosted with optional AI.



- **[OpenGP](https://github.com/vivan1211/opengp)**  

  Open-source AI intelligence platform for private equity—RAG over data rooms, portfolio analytics, meeting transcription; self-hosted (AGPL).



- **[Cap table & fund economics open tools (Hemrock ecosystem)](https://www.hemrock.com/)**  

  Open modeling tools for ownership, waterfalls, and fund returns used by founders and emerging managers.



- **[ERPNext / open ERP for fund-like entities](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open ERP sometimes adapted for SPV accounting and simple investment entity tracking.



- **[Deal flow CRM on open stacks](https://github.com/search?q=deal+flow+OR+venture+CRM+open+source)**  

  Community CRM configurations (e.g. Twenty, EspoCRM) customized for pipeline tracking.



- **[LP reporting template projects](https://github.com/search?q=LP+report+OR+ILPA+template+open+source)**  

  Spreadsheet and document templates aligned to ILPA-style reporting (process, not full software).



- **[Portfolio monitoring notebooks](https://github.com/search?q=private+equity+portfolio+monitoring+OR+MOIC+IRR)**  

  Academic and practitioner open analysis for IRR, MOIC, and cohort metrics.



- **[Document/data-room open stacks](https://github.com/search?q=virtual+data+room+open+source)**  

  Open document management used as lightweight data rooms for smaller funds.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Fund ops**: Portfolio/Hemrock-style self-hosted GP platforms.

- **PE intelligence**: OpenGP for document RAG and portfolio analytics.

- **Modeling**: Open cap table and waterfall tools for early-stage funds.

- Commercial platforms remain essential for regulated fundraising, complex waterfall admin, and institutional LP portals.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

**Portfolio** and **OpenGP** are the main open product-like options for fund ops and PE intelligence.  

Commercial platforms (Juniper Square, Allvue, Dynamo, Backstop, Chronograph, iCapital, AngelList, etc.) dominate institutional workflows.  

Emerging managers may start with open tools and spreadsheets; established GPs and wealth platforms use commercial systems. Fully open alternatives infrastructure is still early relative to SaaS maturity.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Alternative investments are heavily regulated (securities law, fiduciary duty, AML). Software does not replace legal, compliance, or fund-admin expertise. Incorrect capital account or waterfall calculations can cause serious disputes.

- Open-source tools offer data ownership but require operational and compliance ownership. Commercial platforms shift product and often compliance tooling to the vendor. Always involve qualified counsel and administrators.
